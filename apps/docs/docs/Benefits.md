## Let's go

1. You can create multiple version of your resume in minutes.
2. If the job portal supports central resume, you never need to re-create your profile on the job portals.
3. You can share your resume with a single link without worrying about updates because it always up to date.
4. No need to deal with file formats, templates, or design.

## How can a Recruiters or company benefit?

1. Efficient candidate shorting and filtering because we use structured data and AI better understand it.
2. Easy candidate screening process without dealing with files. just use api or our dashboard.
